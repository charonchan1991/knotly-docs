# What is Knotly

[Knotly](https://www.knotly.com/) is an online workspace for designing, visualizing, and organizing crochet and amigurumi patterns. It brings written instructions and 3D previews together through real-time physics simulations so that makers can explore a pattern before reaching for their yarn.

Development on Knotly began in early 2025. After more than a year of dedicated work, Knotly's physics engine is finally ready to meet the world and be put to the test by real crocheters.

In short, Knotly can take a text-based crochet pattern like this:

```
R1: 8X
R2: 8V
R3: 8 (X, V)
R4: 6 (3X, V)
R5: 6 (2X, V, 2X)
R6: 6 (5X, V)
R7-R15: 42X
R16: 6 (5X, A)
R17: 6 (2X, A, 2X)
R18: 6 (3X, A)
R19: 8 (X, A)
R20: 8A
```

and then render the pattern into these 3d models that you play around with:

## Why Knotly

Crochet ideas can be difficult to evaluate from written instructions alone, especially amigurumi projects that take shape in three dimensions. Knotly lets you preview a pattern in 3D before investing your yarn, time, and effort. Experiment virtually with stitch counts, shaping, colors, and construction to identify potential issues early, compare variations quickly, and refine your design with confidence.

By helping you make informed decisions before picking up your hook, Knotly can reduce unnecessary frogging, rework, and wasted materials while making patterns easier to understand and share. Whether you’re casually experimenting or working on a serious project, you will find Knotly a powerful and reliable design companion.

What sets Knotly apart from similar apps and platforms is the care behind it. While many recent projects are rushed to market in pursuit of quick results, Knotly is built with sustained dedication, thoughtful engineering, and genuine craftsmanship. It is a carefully developed product shaped by real people — not a hastily assembled, AI-generated experiment.

Knotly’s physics engine produces realistic and accurate previews that reflect real-world shaping, whether you are designing amigurumi figures, motifs, granny squares, or flowing lace patterns. It is very fast, too: Knotly can simulate many patterns in near real time, allowing you to see changes almost as soon as you make them.

## How Knotly works

Knotly reads the pattern instructions you enter and translates them into a connected stitch model. As you edit rounds or rows, or sew pieces or join parts together, the 3D visual preview updates to show how each change affects the project’s shape and structure.

While Knotly uses rigorous physics-based simulation to produce the most realistic results possible, the preview is a design aid rather than a substitute for making a gauge swatch or testing a finished pattern. In reality, yarn, hook size, tension, and individual technique can all affect the final result.

## What you can do with Knotly

- Create and organize crochet projects
- Write patterns using concise stitch notation of your choice
- Turn text-based crochet patterns into interactive 3D models
- Test out an AI-generated crochet pattern before reaching for your yarn
- Preview your work as a chart, blueprint, or realistic model in 3D
- Made adjustment to colors, shaping, and presentation
- Explore stitches of your work 
- Assemble multiple parts in a single project
- Share your crochet projects and ideas with friends in style
- and more for you to discover!

## Is Knotly hard to learn?
It may feel a little daunting at first, but once you get the hang of it, using Knotly can feel as natural as crocheting itself — especially if you already have experience with text-based crochet patterns. There is a bit of a learning curve if you are new to the crochet world, but practice makes perfect.

Before getting started, familiarize yourself with Knotly’s instruction language first. Knotly uses a domain-specific language (DSL) whose commands are largely based on crochet terminology widely recognized by crocheters around the world. If you haven’t already, take a look at [Instruction Language: Fundamentals](/docs/dsl) for a crash course.
